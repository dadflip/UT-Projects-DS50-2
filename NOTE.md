# Note on Project Origins

This project is a continuation of research work originally conducted by **Linda Chouati** ([@p1805862](https://forge.univ-lyon1.fr/p1805862)) at Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1.

The original work laid the foundations for this XAI (Explainable Artificial Intelligence) interface, and I am deeply grateful for the quality and rigor of that initial research.

## Acknowledgements

A sincere thank you to **Linda Chouati** for her pioneering work on this project. Her contributions provided a solid and well-structured base that made it possible to continue and expand this research.

## Continuation & Improvements

This repository picks up where the original work left off. The goals going forward include:

- Improving the existing interface and user experience
- Extending XAI method coverage
- Enhancing model compatibility and performance
- Adding documentation and reproducibility

All original contributions are credited to their author. Any new additions and modifications are made in the spirit of open, collaborative research.
